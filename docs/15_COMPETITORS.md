# 15 Competitors, and the gap we fill

What we found in a desk search in November 2026. None of it was tested hands-on behind a
login, so treat the "gap" column as our reading, not as proof.

| Product | What it does | What Thomas still misses |
|---|---|---|
| EU TARIC consultation | the official duties per product code and origin | no watchlist of his producers, no alert, no euros on his lines |
| Access2Markets | official trade information for traders | the same |
| hsciq.com table of EU measures | an unofficial table of EU rates by product code | no watchlist, no alert, no published error rate |
| EY Official Journal digests | people write lists of new acts, tagged with product codes | a newsletter for everyone, not per supplier |
| Global Trade Alert in AI assistants | answers questions over a database of trade measures | a tool to ask, not one that warns; no company rates |
| Tandom, Harmonize | catalogues and digests of US duties | US only |
| AEB, Descartes (paid) | large trade compliance suites | may cover parts of this behind a login `[VERIFY]` |

## The gap

Nothing we found combines these three:

1. a watchlist of the importer's own **producers** against the EU's company rates;
2. **euros** on the importer's own purchase lines;
3. a **published error rate** for the reading of the acts.

That is our open space. It is medium-sized, not empty.

## How Thomas would say it

"TARIC tells me the rate once I know which row is mine. Nobody tells me, on the day the act
comes out, that one of my producers is in it, under a slightly different spelling, and
what that would cost me."
